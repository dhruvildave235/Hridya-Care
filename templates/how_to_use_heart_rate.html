❤️ Heart Rate Measurement

Follow these steps for accurate and reliable results

1

Relax before starting

Sit calmly for at least 5 minutes. Avoid activity, stress, or walking before measurement.

2

Warm your finger

If your finger feels cold, gently rub your hands for 20–30 seconds to improve blood flow.

3

Ensure good lighting

Stay in a well-lit area. If flash is used, do not cover the camera improperly.

4

Keep still

Place your finger gently on the camera. Avoid movement, pressure, or talking.

5

Wait for completion

Remain steady for 20–30 seconds until the progress indicator finishes.

6

Review trends

Single readings vary. Track multiple readings over time for meaningful insights.

🧠 Why these steps improve accuracy

+

Camera-based rPPG detects tiny color changes caused by blood flow. Movement, cold skin, stress, and poor lighting reduce signal quality. These steps improve signal stability and accuracy.

🩺 When NOT to measure

  • Immediately after exercise
  • When hands are extremely cold or shaking
  • During fever, illness, or dehydration
  • While walking, talking, or lying down
  • If camera lens is dirty or damaged
This feature provides wellness insights using camera-based sensing. It is not intended for medical diagnosis.
