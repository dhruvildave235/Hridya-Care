Terms & Conditions

These Terms govern your use of HridyaCare—a wellness-focused platform utilizing rPPG technology to provide physiological insights.

1. Nature & Purpose of HridyaCare

HridyaCare is designed to support wellness awareness, self-monitoring, and health education. The platform analyzes subtle optical changes in facial skin captured via a camera to estimate physiological trends such as heart rate.

The system is intended for informational and educational use and should not be interpreted as a clinical, diagnostic, or therapeutic tool.

2. Scientific Basis & Accuracy

HridyaCare utilizes remote photoplethysmography (rPPG), a non-contact technique widely studied in academic research.

Camera-based heart-rate estimates typically have a margin of error of ±8 to ±10 BPM compared to clinical-grade sensors.

Accuracy depends on several external variables:

  • Environmental lighting and shadows
  • User movement and facial expressions
  • Camera resolution and frame rate stability
  • Physical obstructions (makeup, facial hair, masks)

3. Medical & Clinical Disclaimer

HridyaCare is not a medical device. It has not been clinically validated for emergency assessment or treatment decisions. Never ignore professional medical advice because of something you have read on this platform.

4. User Responsibilities

  • Ensure measurements are taken in a well-lit, quiet environment.
  • Remain still for the duration of the scan for optimal results.
  • Understand that insights are trends, not medical absolutes.
  • Use the data for personal wellness curiosity only.

5. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, HridyaCare and its developers shall not be liable for any harm, medical emergencies, or decisions made based on the wellness insights provided by the software.
