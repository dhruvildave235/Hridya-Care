Heart Health

Resting Heart Rate Explained

Your resting heart rate (RHR) is the number of times your heart beats per minute while you are at complete rest—usually measured right after you wake up.

[Image of heart conduction system]

What is Normal?
For most adults, a healthy range is between 60 and 100 beats per minute (BPM).

Did you know? Athletes often have lower RHRs (40–60 BPM) because their heart muscle is more efficient at pumping blood.

Why lower is usually better:
A lower resting heart rate implies better cardiovascular fitness and more efficient heart function.

Factors affecting RHR:

  • Stress and anxiety
  • Medication
  • Hydration levels
  • Physical activity level
