Best Foods for Heart Health

What you eat has a direct impact on your blood pressure, cholesterol, and overall heart resilience. Here are the top foods to include in your diet.

🥜
Walnuts & Almonds
Rich in Omega-3 and healthy fats that lower bad cholesterol.
🫐
Berries
Strawberries and blueberries are packed with antioxidants.
🐟
Fatty Fish
Salmon and tuna are loaded with Omega-3s to reduce triglycerides.
🥦
Leafy Greens
Spinach and kale contain Vitamin K, which protects your arteries.
